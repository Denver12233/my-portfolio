## Overview
Starting month two with a focus on interactivity. I spent the week implementing advanced animations using Framer Motion to make the portfolio feel more alive and responsive.

## Day 1 — Entrance Sequences
Developed staggered entrance animations for our grid layouts. I wanted to ensure that items reveal themselves in a way that feels organic and guides the user's eye.

## Day 2 — Scroll-Triggered Effects
Implemented scroll-triggered animations for the homepage sections. Using the `whileInView` prop, we've added subtle parallax effects that enhance the depth of the interface.

## Day 3 — Micro-interactions
Focused on micro-interactions for buttons and links. I added hover and tap effects that provide immediate visual feedback, significantly improving the tactility of the UI.

## Day 4 — Layout Animations
Explored Framer Motion's `layout` prop to handle smooth transitions when items are filtered or reordered. This was particularly challenging but resulted in a much more polished experience.

## Day 5 — Performance Tweak
Spent the day profiling our animations to ensure they aren't dropping frames. I optimized some complex SVG animations to use hardware acceleration.

## Key Takeaways
- Subtle animations can significantly elevate the premium feel of a site.
- Scroll-triggered effects should be used sparingly to avoid overwhelming the user.
- Performance profiling is essential when using high-fidelity animations.

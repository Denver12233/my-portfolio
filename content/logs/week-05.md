## Overview

Week 5 continued the momentum from the previous week, with a focus on cohort management features, system-wide profile and logo integration, and improving how administrators communicate with users through an announcement system. Each day built on the last, resulting in a more complete and polished platform by the end of the week.

## Day-by-Day

**March 9 — Cohort Functionality for TBI Coach and UniAdmin**

The week started with adding cohort functionality to both the TBI Coach and UniAdmin sides of the system. Cohorts are a core organizational unit of the platform, and making sure both roles can properly manage and interact with them was an important step toward a fully functional multi-role system.

**March 10 — Cohort Batch Dropdown and Announcement Modal**

I built the cohort batch dropdown, which allows users to select and filter by specific cohort batches — making it easier to manage groups of students across different time periods. Alongside this, I implemented the Send Announcement modal within the UniAdmin interface, giving university administrators a direct way to broadcast messages to their users.

**March 11 — Ecosystem and Institution Profile Integration**

This was a full-stack day — I updated the ecosystem and institution sections of the platform by integrating logos and profiles for each institution. This required handling both the frontend design to display the data correctly and the backend data fetching to pull the right information from the database. Seeing institutions represented with their actual logos and profiles made the platform feel significantly more real and production-ready.

**March 12 — System-Wide Profile and Logo Display**

Building on the previous day's work, I extended the profile and logo display across all roles and institutions throughout the entire system. Every role — from students to coaches to admins — now has their profile displayed consistently, and every institution shows its logo wherever it appears. This kind of visual consistency is important for making the platform feel cohesive and trustworthy to its users.

**March 13 — Send Announcement Feature in SuperAdmin**

The week closed with implementing the Send Announcement feature in the SuperAdmin dashboard and improving the overall notifications system. SuperAdmins can now broadcast announcements platform-wide, and the notification system was refined to ensure messages are delivered and displayed clearly for better communication and user experience.

## Key Takeaways

- Added cohort management functionality for both TBI Coach and UniAdmin roles
- Built cohort batch dropdown for easier filtering and management
- Implemented the Send Announcement modal in UniAdmin and SuperAdmin
- Integrated institution logos and profiles across the ecosystem with full-stack implementation
- Improved the platform's notifications system for better communication

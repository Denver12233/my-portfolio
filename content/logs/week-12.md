## Overview

Week 12 was a focused and detail-oriented week dedicated almost entirely to the internship portfolio project. With no new tickets coming in from the system for most of the week, the time was well spent refining and polishing the portfolio — improving its functionality, layout, and user interface responsiveness — ultimately preparing it for final deployment.

## Day-by-Day

**May 4 — Portfolio Functionality and Layout Enhancement**

The week kicked off with enhancements to the professional portfolio's functionality and layout. This involved revisiting existing sections, tightening the overall structure, and ensuring the portfolio presented work in a clear and compelling way — a strong foundation to build on for the rest of the week.

**May 5 — Continued Portfolio Development While Awaiting New Tickets**

With no new tickets or tasks arriving in the system, the day was spent continuing development on the portfolio. This uninterrupted time was valuable — it allowed for a deeper focus on content and flow without context-switching, ensuring steady progress on the project.

**May 6 — UI Design Refinement and Responsiveness Improvements**

The focus shifted to visual polish — refining the portfolio's design and improving user interface responsiveness across different screen sizes. Responsive design is critical for a portfolio, as it needs to look great whether viewed on a desktop, tablet, or mobile device. Careful attention was given to layout breakpoints, spacing, and component behavior at various viewports.

**May 7 — Final Review and Pre-Deployment Polish**

The week closed with a thorough review of the entire portfolio project. This involved revisiting every section, catching any remaining inconsistencies, and making final adjustments — all in preparation for deployment. Getting a project to a deployable state requires a different kind of attention than building it: everything needs to feel intentional, complete, and ready for a real audience.

## Key Takeaways

- Enhanced the professional portfolio's functionality and overall layout structure
- Maintained steady development progress during downtime between system tickets
- Refined the portfolio's UI design for a more polished and cohesive visual experience
- Improved responsiveness across multiple screen sizes and device types
- Conducted a final review and applied pre-deployment polish to the portfolio project
- Prepared the internship portfolio for final deployment as a capstone of the OJT journey

## Overview

Week 9 was a pivotal one — it marked both the closing chapter of the first system and the beginning of something new. The week started with finalizing and handing over the completed platform to the client, then quickly shifted gears as the team was onboarded onto PromptGraph, a new system project. The latter half of the week was spent exploring the new codebase and conducting data audits to understand the current state of the system.

## Day-by-Day

**April 13 — Core Mentor Dashboard Implementation and Profile Sheets**

The week opened with implementing the core of the Mentor Dashboard — bringing together real-time chat, activity tracking, and profile management into a unified interface. Alongside this, I created and integrated mentor profile sheets designed for admin review, giving administrators a structured way to evaluate and manage mentor information from within the platform.

**April 15 — Beta Testing, Debugging, and System Handover**

With the platform now feature-complete, this day was dedicated to debugging and beta testing the entire system end-to-end. After verifying that everything was working as expected, the system was officially handed over to the client. It was a significant milestone — seeing months of work come together into a finished, deliverable product.

**April 16 — Onboarding to PromptGraph**

With the first project wrapped up, the team was onboarded onto PromptGraph, a new system project. This day was spent getting familiar with the new codebase, understanding its structure, and exploring how the existing system was built — laying the groundwork for the contributions to come.

**April 17 — Data Audit and Client Link Setup**

Diving deeper into PromptGraph, I began creating an audit to identify differences and inconsistencies in the data. This involved examining the client side of the system, auditing the data it held, and setting up a link to better connect and track the information across the platform.

**April 18 — Integration Audit and Data Health Check**

Building on the previous day's audit work, I conducted a thorough Integration Audit and Data Health check across the system. This involved identifying clients with missing required data — a critical step toward ensuring PromptGraph's data integrity before any further development could proceed.

## Key Takeaways

- Completed the core Mentor Dashboard with real-time chat, activity tracking, and profile management
- Created and integrated mentor profile sheets for admin review
- Successfully debugged, beta tested, and handed over the completed system to the client
- Onboarded onto PromptGraph as the team's new system project
- Conducted data audits to identify inconsistencies and clients with missing required data
- Laid the groundwork for data integrity improvements in PromptGraph

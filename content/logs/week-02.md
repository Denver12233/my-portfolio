## Overview
This week was focused on laying the foundation for our UI library. I worked on building the primary navigation and core button components using Atomic Design principles.

## Day 1 — Primary Navigation
Started the week by sketching and then implementing the primary navigation system. I focused on making it responsive and ensuring smooth transitions between different sections of the app.

## Day 2 — Atomic Atoms
Began creating the smallest building blocks: buttons, inputs, and typography tokens. Following Atomic Design principles, I focused on making these atoms flexible and composable.

## Day 3 — Building Molecules
Combined atoms into more complex molecules, such as the search bar and card headers. Tailwind CSS has made styling these components extremely efficient and consistent.

## Day 4 — Accessibility Research
Spent time researching WCAG standards to ensure our components are accessible. I focused particularly on the dark mode toggle and ensuring color contrast passes the AA level.

## Day 5 — Theme Integration & Review
Finalized the theme toggle and integrated it across the initial set of components. It's rewarding to see the UI start to come together with these small but essential pieces.

## Key Takeaways
- Atomic Design significantly improves component reusability.
- Early focus on accessibility prevents major refactors later.
- Tailwind's utility-first approach speeds up the design-to-code workflow.

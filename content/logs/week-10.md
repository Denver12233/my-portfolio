## Overview

Week 10 was a focused week of quality work — hunting down and fixing data display issues, pushing system development forward, and then shifting into audit mode for the AI Tracker. By the end of the week, the team had a clearer picture of the bugs present in the system and had begun refactoring how query results were structured and displayed.

## Day-by-Day

**April 21 — Fixing Inaccurate Data Display**

The week began by continuing the work of correcting inaccurately displaying data within the system. Getting the data to display correctly is foundational — no matter how good the features are, users lose trust in a platform that shows them wrong information, so this was a high-priority fix.

**April 22 — Continued Data Display Corrections**

The data display issues required more than a single day to fully resolve, so April 22 was spent continuing those fixes. Persistent bugs like these often require careful tracing through both the frontend rendering logic and the backend data sources to pinpoint the exact cause and apply a reliable solution.

**April 23 — Continued System Development**

With the data display issues under control, the focus shifted back to general system development — moving PromptGraph forward and building out the next set of features and improvements as the project continued to take shape.

**April 24 — AI Tracker Bug Audit**

This day marked the beginning of a dedicated audit of the AI Tracker. The goal was to systematically identify, document, and track bugs present in the system — creating a clear record of what needed to be fixed and setting the stage for targeted resolution in the days ahead.

**April 25 — AI Tracker Audit Continuation and Result Ordering Refactor**

The audit continued into April 25, with additional bugs identified and documented. Alongside the audit, I refactored the result ordering logic in the AI Tracker to group queries together and display their responses consecutively — making the output significantly easier to read and interpret for users reviewing AI-generated results.

## Key Takeaways

- Resolved inaccurate data display issues affecting the reliability of the system
- Continued pushing PromptGraph system development forward
- Conducted a thorough audit of the AI Tracker to identify, document, and track bugs
- Refactored result ordering to group queries and display responses consecutively for better readability

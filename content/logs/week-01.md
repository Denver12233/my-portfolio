## Overview
My first week at MakerSpace InnovHub (MIH) has been an intensive introduction to the team's workflow and technology stack. I focused on setting up my local environment and understanding the architectural principles of our main projects.

## Day 1 — Orientation & Environment Setup
The day started with a team orientation where I met my mentor and the rest of the development team. I successfully cloned the core repositories and spent the afternoon configuring my VS Code environment, installing dependencies, and ensuring all environment variables were correctly set.

## Day 2 — First Task Assigned
I was assigned my first task: a small UI refinement for the dashboard. This gave me the opportunity to dive into the codebase and understand the component structure. I spent time reading through the internal documentation regarding the Atomic Design system we use.

## Day 3 — Deep Work
Focused on implementing the UI refinement using Tailwind CSS and Framer Motion. I had to ensure that the animations were smooth and adhered to the design team's specifications in Figma. I also participated in a code review session for a senior developer's PR.

## Day 4 — Progress & Blockers
Completed the UI refinement and pushed my first branch. I encountered a minor blocker with a hydration mismatch in one of the client components, which I resolved by correctly using the `useEffect` hook to handle client-side-only rendering.

## Day 5 — Week Wrap-up & Reflection
I wrapped up the week by documenting my progress and preparing for the next set of tasks. This week taught me a lot about the importance of a clean development environment and the benefits of following a structured design system.

## Key Takeaways
- Importance of documenting environment setup for new joiners.
- Deeper understanding of Next.js hydration and client-side rendering.
- The value of Atomic Design in maintaining a scalable codebase.
